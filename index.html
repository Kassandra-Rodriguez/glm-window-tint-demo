Tint levels

See the shade before we cut it

Tint darkness is measured as VLT, how much visible light gets through. A lower number means darker glass. Tap a level to preview it on a side window.

35% VLT · medium

How dark?

  • 70% to 50% is nearly clear. It blocks heat and UV with almost no darkness, the usual pick for windshields.
  • 35% is medium. A visible tint you can still see out of clearly at night.
  • 20% to 5% is dark to limo. Maximum privacy, darkest on the back glass.

Texas limits how dark front side windows can be. We tint to your spec and go over what's street legal when you come in. Confirm current law for your vehicle.
